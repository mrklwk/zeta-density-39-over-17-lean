# Third-party provenance

The source notices and original attribution in the frozen Lean files are retained verbatim. Exact upstream notice texts and all nine dependency revisions are preserved under `licensing/`, including OpenAI/math and its retained adaptations, Mathlib, PNT+, McColm, and the other sources recorded there. Historical comparison labels are bibliographic credits, not claims to have reimplemented every original historical argument.

Distinct upstream MIT, MIT-0, Apache-2.0 and other notices remain in force and must be preserved.

Detailed source attribution and adaptation records from the preceding public release are preserved in [licensing/previous_public_release/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](licensing/previous_public_release/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), with the original license texts under licenses/. Its lean/ paths refer to the preceding layout; current Lean modules are at the repository root.
